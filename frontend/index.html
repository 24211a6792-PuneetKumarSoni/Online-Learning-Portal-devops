About the Platform

Pathshala bridges the gap between classrooms and homes, establishing a seamless digital network for holistic development and clear communication.

Bridging Learning & Monitoring

We believe education thrives when teachers, students, and parents collaborate in harmony. Pathshala provides a unified space where learning materials are shared instantly, grading is transparent, and student achievements are tracked in real-time.

Our robust design supports detailed study guides, video lecture repositories, online quizzes, fee receipt records, and automated notifications so that nothing goes unnoticed.
